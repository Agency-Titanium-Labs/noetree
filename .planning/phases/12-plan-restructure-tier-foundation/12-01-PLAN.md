<objective>
Remove the Free-tier 20-note cap and every piece of code, UI, and copy that existed only to enforce or explain it (D-04, PLAN-06).

Purpose: PLAN-06 requires notes to be unlimited on every plan. The server-side check in `convex/notes.ts` is the enforcement point; the `UpgradeModal` stack, the two `NOTE_LIMIT_REACHED` client catch branches, and the `PlanEnforcement` i18n namespace are downstream consumers that become dead code the instant the check is gone. They are deleted in lockstep, not disabled behind a flag.

Output: `createNote` accepts an unbounded number of notes per user; `UpgradeModal.tsx` and `UpgradeModalProvider.tsx` no longer exist; `convex/notes.test.ts` proves the unlimited behaviour instead of asserting the cap.
</objective>
