action right after `stripe.accounts.create`, storing only an inert account id) and
`upsertFromStripeAccount` (the webhook-driven internal mutation that is the ONLY writer of

exclusively from a signature-verified Stripe event (07-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 1), the mutation must be
replay-safe, must handle the first-event-before-our-own-write race (Pitfall 4), and must refuse to
hand one user's Stripe account capabilities to a different user.
Output: two new mutations in `convex/connectAccounts.ts` plus their tests.
