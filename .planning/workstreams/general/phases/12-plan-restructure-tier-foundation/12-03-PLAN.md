<objective>
Generalize the boolean `isProUser`/`isPro` pattern into a single server-side `getUserTier(ctx): "free" | "premium"` source of truth exposed to clients as one Convex query, and rebrand the Settings and AI-credits surfaces from "Pro" to "Premium".

Purpose: D-03 locks this signature, and phases 15 (version-history retention) and 16 (AI credit quotas) both key off this same entitlement — they must not each re-derive it. 12-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 4 flags the concrete drift risk: `SettingsPlanCard.tsx` and `CreditsExhaustedDialog.tsx` each independently compute `subscription?.status === "active"` today, so any future change to what counts as "premium" (e.g. a `past_due` grace period) would silently miss them.

Output: `getUserTier` + `UserTier` in `convex/helpers/helper.ts`, a `getSubscriptionTier` query in `convex/subscriptions.ts`, both client components consuming it, and the `Settings`/`AiCredits` i18n namespaces rebranded in English and French.
</objective>
