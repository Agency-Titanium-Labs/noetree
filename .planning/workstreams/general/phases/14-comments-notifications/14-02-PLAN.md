<objective>
Implement the comment backend: reactive list/count queries usable by anonymous visitors, add (with transactional notification fan-out), resolve/unresolve, delete with tombstones, and the note-deletion cascade.

Purpose: Delivers COMMENT-01..04 and NOTIF-01 server-side; plan 14-06 renders these functions, plan 14-03 reads the notifications they create.
Deliberate trade-off (keep, and restate in 14-02-SUMMARY under "Decisions"): non-owner recipients for whom getRecipientAccess returns null (lost access, or link-only access) are skipped. D-12 says the root author is notified on replies; this narrows it for link-only authors because a notification for them could only link to a route that rejects them or to the share token itself.
Output: `convex/comments.ts`, `deleteCommentsForNote` in the helper, `deleteNote` hook, test suites "access", "projection", "thread", "resolve", "delete", "cascade", "survives", "recipients".
