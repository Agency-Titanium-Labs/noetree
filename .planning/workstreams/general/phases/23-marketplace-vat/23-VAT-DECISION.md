## Open items for the accountant

1. Single French rate vs buyer-country rate.
2. Commission on post-VAT amount (this draft) vs on the VAT-inclusive price.
3. Who is the deemed supplier; OSS registration; receipts/invoices to buyers.
4. Refund/chargeback VAT reversal (no refund path exists today; manual runbook).

5. No refund path exists in the product: a refund or chargeback does not reverse the VAT carve-out, the platform commission or the `sellerEarnings` counters. Until the accountant defines the treatment, handle refunds manually (runbook below).

## Manual refund runbook (interim)

1. Refund in the Stripe Dashboard with `refund_application_fee` and `reverse_transfer` enabled so the seller transfer and the full application fee (VAT + commission) are returned.
2. Mark the purchase and its delivery handling manually (no automated revocation exists).
3. Correct the seller's `sellerEarnings` counters by hand or with `npx convex run sales:backfillEarnings` after adjusting the purchase row; record the VAT reversal for the accountant.
4. Keep a note per refund (purchase id, amount, VAT part) for the next VAT return.
